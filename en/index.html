01↗

Entrepreneurs · Incubators · Programmes

Move your entrepreneurial project forward

An idea to test. An offer to refine. Next steps to choose.

Formats and expected outcomes ＋

You have an idea or an early-stage business and need to choose your next step.

We help clarify the problem you address, understand future customers, develop your value proposition and test important assumptions. The support structures your trials and field learning so you can continue, adjust or redirect your project.

Formats

  • A focused individual session.
  • A coaching journey with fieldwork between sessions.
  • A programme combining group workshops and individual support, designed with an incubator, research organisation or entrepreneurship programme.

What you take away Clearer assumptions, documented learning and practical next steps.

02↗

Business leaders · Project owners

Clarify the situation and choose what comes next

A problem to understand. Priorities to decide. A direction to take.

Formats and expected outcomes ＋

You can see what no longer works, and need to understand where to intervene.

Information gets lost. Responsibilities are unclear. A service needs to change. We examine a defined problem, service or journey with the people involved, observe practices and make the current working process understandable. Together we develop priorities and options suited to your resources.

Formats

  • A targeted review of one process or service.
  • Interviews, observation and collaborative workshops.
  • A shared diagnosis, options and an actionable plan.

What you take away A shared understanding, a map of current operations, priorities and an action plan. This can stand alone or prepare implementation.

Let’s discuss this support ↗
03↗

Business leaders · Operational teams

Organise work and equip your team

Information to connect. Roles to clarify. Tools to put into use.

Formats and expected outcomes ＋

You want a more reliable way of working that your team can maintain.

We support a first operational scope: clarify steps, distribute responsibilities, organise information and create or adapt useful tools. A shared workspace, form, dashboard, automation or AI may help. We choose the means with you and test them with the team.

Formats

  • A defined operational process and clear responsibilities.
  • Configured tools, tested with the people using them.
  • Useful documentation, training and handover.

What you take away An improved process, configured tools and a team supported in taking ownership. Further rollout can be decided from initial use.

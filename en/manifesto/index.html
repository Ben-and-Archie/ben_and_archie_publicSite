BEN & ARCHIE

The Ben & Archie manifesto

We believe in ideas that meet the field, and changes that take shape in everyday work.

A business is built through convictions, questions and trials. It moves forward when its teams understand the problems to solve, can make choices and have the means to put them into practice.

Ben & Archie exists to support that movement. We are a consulting and support collective. We help entrepreneurs build their ventures and leaders of small and medium-sized organisations develop their businesses.

Start with what really happens

An idea to test. A service to rethink. Requests lost across several channels. Information that is difficult to find. An organisation relying too heavily on a few people.

We start with these practical situations. We listen, observe and seek to understand work as it happens. We question habits and assumptions, and identify together what should change and what already works.

Give ideas the means to prove themselves

For early-stage entrepreneurs, we provide a framework to clarify a project, understand future customers, work on the business model and test assumptions. For evolving businesses, we bring an outside perspective and a method for imagining new responses, reorganising work and implementing suitable solutions.

We move through stages: clarify, choose, test, learn and implement. Trials can confirm a direction, open another or lead to stopping an idea. A well-supported decision is a useful outcome.

Choose means that fit the need

A clearer responsibility can unlock a team. A shared process can make customer follow-up more reliable. A digital tool can make information accessible. Automation or AI can help a task when its usefulness is established.

We choose these means with you, according to your activity, resources and the people who will use them. For a property agency, this may improve tenant-request tracking. In a workshop, it may make maintenance history accessible. For a young business, it may help test an offer with its first customers.

Change must be understandable, usable and sustainable in everyday work.

Build with the people involved

Your knowledge of the business is essential. So is that of your teams, customers and partners. We create the conditions to combine these perspectives, surface useful disagreements and make clear decisions. This requires availability, openness and willingness to try.

We contribute structure, questions, skills and the ability to make things happen.

Leave the means to continue

Each assignment should leave an outcome you can use: a decision, test, organisation or tool. We also prepare what comes next: explicit responsibilities, useful documentation and a team able to take over. Knowledge transfer is part of the work.

Our ambition: help you make sound choices, make them real and continue with greater independence.

Ben & Archie — understand, choose, test, make it happen.

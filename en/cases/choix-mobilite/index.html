User research and decision support · Arthur’s experience at Schoolab for a mobility company

The situation

A company wanted to understand how passengers perceived an innovation intended to improve their on-board experience, and whether it could influence booking choices. Before a possible rollout, the team needed to test the proposition with users.

Arthur’s contribution

As part of a Schoolab assignment, Arthur contributed to market research, interviews, an international survey and desirability testing. A simulated booking journey was designed to observe understanding and choices in a test setting.

The outcome

In the documented prototype results, 121 of the 151 participants who completed the journey selected the proposed option. This finding concerns choices within a simulation. It does not measure real purchases or commercial adoption.

A Ben & Archie product · Functional prototype

The need we explore

A tenant reports a problem through messages and a photo. The property manager needs to link it to the right home, complete the information and track it through resolution. NestFix explores a simple journey for this handover and follow-up.

What we are developing

The prototype guides reporting through Telegram, structures the information and organises the related files. Requests are connected to property data and an operational workspace, with media and statuses attached to the record.

What has been demonstrated

Reporting journeys with photos and tracking have been demonstrated, and technical components have been tested. The next step is to assess usefulness in a real rental-management setting. Time savings, adoption and user satisfaction remain to be measured.

This illustrates our approach: start with an everyday situation, build a working journey, then test it in use before extending it.

B&A assignment · Organisation & tools

Let’s talk about your project ↗

Organisation and digital tools · A Ben & Archie assignment for an anonymised property business

The situation

Managing property purchases and sales involves properties, documents, deadlines, partners and tasks. The business wanted to bring scattered information together and structure its operational follow-up.

Our contribution

Ben & Archie scoped and structured a Notion-based management system around the activities and decisions to track. We defined relationships between business entities, documented purchase and sales processes, and organised checklists and views for different roles. A user manual explained the structure, navigation and workflows.

The outcome

The assignment produced a documented management foundation: an architecture connecting essential information and reference points for following each operation. It illustrates how process clarification and tool design can develop together. Time savings and adoption remain to be measured.

Do your operations rely on scattered information and constant follow-ups? We help organise tracking and build tools suited to your team.

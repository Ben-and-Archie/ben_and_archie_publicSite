Ben & Archie’s internal laboratory · Architecture in development

The need we explore

How can we retain control over AI assistants as models, providers and their terms evolve? Hermoss aims to make assistants LLM-agnostic: their organisation, memory and knowledge should remain reusable across different AI models.

What we are building

We are working to separate assistants’ roles, instructions, knowledge, tools and data from the model running them. The goal is to change to another local or remote LLM without rebuilding the assistant or losing its working context. We aim for a frictionless transition, preserving continuity and limiting migration work.

This independence includes control over data: where it is stored and processed, who can access it, and how it is backed up, restored or moved.

The independence we seek

We aim for full autonomy in choosing and evolving the system: selecting suitable models, retaining useful information and continuing work without dependence on a single provider. Assistants’ responsibilities and actions requiring human approval remain defined for each use.

What is documented

An architecture and allocation of responsibilities have been documented. Connections and interfaces have undergone targeted technical checks. Complete portability between LLMs, continuity during migration and data restoration remain objectives to assess through dedicated tests.

Our ambition: controllable, adaptable assistants, with freedom to change models and retain control of your data.

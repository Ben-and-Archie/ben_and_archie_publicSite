Entrepreneurial coaching · A Ben & Archie assignment within an IRD support programme

The situation

An AgriBioTech researcher in Cambodia wanted to prepare the application of her research. Which product should take priority, for which users, with what value proposition and next steps?

Our contribution

Ben & Archie helped her connect her scientific ambition with an entrepreneurial path. The work covered product priorities, user segmentation, the value proposition and the business model, alongside sessions on vision, positioning and project identity.

The outcome

The support helped formalise a positioning, the foundations of a brand and a path towards commercial preparation. These provide a framework for further decisions and validation. Company creation and sales remain separate milestones.

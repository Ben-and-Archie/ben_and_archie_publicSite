Field research and service design · Arthur’s experience at Schoolab

The situation

Business complaints arrived through multiple channels, sometimes incomplete or difficult to connect to orders. Customers lacked visibility, and service and sales teams needed repeated exchanges to collect the information required.

Arthur’s contribution

Within a Schoolab team, Arthur contributed to scoping and designing a customer space based on interviews and field observations. The work addressed submission, order association, required information, tracking and notifications. Prototypes helped test and refine interface decisions.

The documented outcome

The approach produced a tested journey and a form designed to gather useful information while remaining readable and connected to the relevant order. It illustrates how observing work can guide tool design and clarify handovers between customers and teams.

Does your team spend too much time completing incoming requests? We help understand the friction and design a more structured journey.
